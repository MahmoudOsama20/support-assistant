# Transfer Limits by Account Tier

## Limits at a glance
Limits are set in Egyptian pounds (EGP) and depend on your verification tier.
- Basic: daily sending limit 5,000, monthly sending limit 20,000, maximum wallet balance 10,000.
- Plus: daily sending limit 30,000, monthly sending limit 100,000, maximum wallet balance 50,000.
- Premium: daily sending limit 150,000, monthly sending limit 500,000, maximum wallet balance 250,000.

## How limits are counted
The daily limit covers all money leaving your wallet in a calendar day: wallet-to-wallet transfers, bank transfers, QR payments and cash withdrawals. The daily counter resets at midnight Cairo time, and the monthly limit resets on the first day of each month. Incoming money does not use up your sending limits, but it does count toward your maximum balance. You can see how much of your daily limit is left on the wallet screen.

## New devices
After you sign in on a new device, transfers are capped at 2,000 EGP for the first 24 hours, whatever your tier. This protects you if someone else obtains your login details.

## Raising your limits
Upgrade your account tier to raise all three limits at once. The new limits apply immediately after your upgrade is approved.
