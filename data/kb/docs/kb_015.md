# Account Closure and Data Deletion

## Requesting closure
You can request to close your account in Settings or through support. Before the account can be closed, its balance must be zero. Any open dispute or unpaid fee also blocks closure, so settle these first and then submit the request again.

## What happens next
Closure is processed within 7 days. A closed account cannot be reopened. If you want to use Nile Wallet again later, you can open a new account with the same national ID.

## Transaction records
Transaction records are kept for 5 years because the law requires it. After that period they are deleted. Closing your account does not shorten this retention period.

## Personal data deletion
Personal data that is not legally required, such as marketing preferences, analytics data and device data, is deleted within 30 days of your request. Only the records that the law obliges us to keep remain until their retention period ends.
