# Fee Schedule 2025 (Superseded)

## Status of this schedule
This fee schedule is superseded. It ended on 2025-12-31 and was replaced by the 2026 fee schedule on 2026-01-01. The figures below are kept for reference only and no longer apply to any transaction.

## Transfer and card fees in 2025
In 2025, an instant bank transfer cost 1% of the amount, with a minimum of 5 EGP and a maximum of 25 EGP. Issuing a physical card cost 50 EGP, and replacing a lost or damaged physical card cost 75 EGP.

## Monthly and international fees in 2025
The monthly fee was 10 EGP for Plus and 30 EGP for Premium. The markup on foreign-currency card payments was 2.5%, and the fee for an international transfer was 2%.

## Which schedule applies now
For current fees, always use the 2026 fee schedule. Do not use the 2025 figures to estimate the cost of anything done on or after 2026-01-01.
