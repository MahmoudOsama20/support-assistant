# Disputes and Chargebacks

## When you can file a dispute
You can file a dispute in the Nile Wallet app within 60 days of the transaction date. Once those 60 days have passed, the transaction can no longer be disputed, so it is worth reviewing your recent card activity regularly and filing as soon as you notice a problem.

## Accepted reasons
Every dispute must be filed under one of four reasons:
- Unauthorized: you did not make or approve the payment.
- Duplicate charge: the same payment was taken more than once.
- Goods or services not received: you paid but never received what you bought.
- Wrong amount: the merchant charged a different amount from the one you agreed to.

## What happens after you file
Nile Wallet contacts the merchant, and the merchant must respond within 10 days. If the disputed amount is up to 5,000 EGP, you receive a provisional credit within 5 business days, so the money is available to you while the case is being reviewed. We issue the final decision within 45 days of the date you filed. Keep any receipts or messages exchanged with the merchant, because they help the review.

## What cannot be disputed
Cash withdrawals and transfers to other people are not eligible for disputes. If you suspect an unauthorized transaction, freeze your card in the app straight away and call the hotline 19800.
