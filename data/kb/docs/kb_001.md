# Account Opening and Identity Verification

## Who can open an account
Anyone aged 18 or over who has an Egyptian national ID and a mobile number can open a Nile Wallet account. Each national ID can be linked to only one account, so a second registration with the same ID is rejected.

## Verification levels
Nile Wallet has three account tiers, and each one needs more proof of identity than the last.
- Basic: enter your mobile number and national ID number. Verification is instant.
- Plus: upload a photo of the front and back of your national ID and take a selfie. The review is completed within 1 business day.
- Premium: everything required for Plus, plus a proof of address (a utility bill that is no older than 3 months) and a source-of-income declaration. The review takes up to 3 business days.

## If verification fails
When a document is rejected, the app shows the reason. You can resubmit your documents up to 3 times within 30 days. After that, please contact support through in-app chat or call the hotline 19800. Clear, well-lit photos without glare are the most common way to avoid a rejection.

## Upgrading later
You can start with a Basic account and upgrade at any time from Profile > Verification. The higher limits of your new tier apply immediately after approval.
