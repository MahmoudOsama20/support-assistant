# Refund Timelines

## Merchant refunds
How long a refund takes depends on where the money is going back to. When a merchant approves a refund to your wallet balance, the money arrives 3-5 business days after the approval. A refund to a linked bank card takes longer, 7-10 business days. For wallet refunds the count starts when the merchant approves, so a request that is still waiting for approval has not started its countdown yet.

## Failed transfers
If a transfer fails, the money is reversed automatically within 24 hours. You do not need to file a request or contact support for this to happen, and the amount returns to the wallet it was sent from.

## Cancelled QR payments
If a QR payment is cancelled, the amount is refunded within 2 business days.

## If your refund is late
When the period for your type of refund has passed and the money has still not arrived, open a chat in the app or write to help@nilewallet.example. Have the transaction date, the amount and the merchant's approval message ready, as this speeds up the check.
