# Merchant and QR Payments

## Paying by QR
Customers can pay a merchant by scanning a QR code in the Nile Wallet app. Paying by QR costs the customer nothing. The amount does count toward the customer's daily limit, so a large purchase reduces how much more can be sent that day. The remaining daily limit is shown on the wallet screen, which is worth checking before a large payment.

## Dynamic and static codes
Merchants can use two kinds of QR code. A dynamic QR code expires after 5 minutes, and the merchant then has to generate a new one if the customer has not paid in time. A static QR code does not expire, so the same code can be scanned again later.

## Merchant fees and settlement
Merchants pay a fee of 1.0% per transaction. Settlement is on the next business day by 6 PM, which means money from a sale is paid out to the merchant the following business day rather than instantly.

## Merchant refunds
A merchant can refund a sale through the merchant dashboard within 30 days of the sale. After 30 days the refund can no longer be made from the dashboard. Once the merchant approves a refund, the money reaches the customer according to the standard refund timelines.
