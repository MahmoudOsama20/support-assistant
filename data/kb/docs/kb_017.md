# International Transfers

## Who can send money abroad
International transfers are available to Plus and Premium accounts. Basic accounts are not eligible, so if you hold a Basic account you need to upgrade first from Profile > Verification. After an upgrade is approved, the limits of the new tier apply. Plus accounts can send up to 20,000 EGP-equivalent per month, and Premium accounts can send up to 100,000 EGP-equivalent per month.

## Currencies and fees
You can send money in USD, EUR, GBP, SAR or AED. The fee for an international transfer is 1.5% of the amount, with a minimum of 50 EGP, which means that even a small transfer pays at least 50 EGP. This fee is separate from the fees of local transfers described in the fee schedule. The fee is shown in the app before you confirm.

## Exchange rate
The exchange rate is shown to you before you confirm the transfer, and it is locked for 60 seconds. Review the amount your recipient will receive within that time, and check the rate again if you need longer.

## What you need and when it arrives
To send money abroad you need the recipient's IBAN/SWIFT details, and you must complete a purpose-of-transfer declaration. Check the IBAN carefully before you confirm, as a wrong detail can delay the transfer. International transfers arrive in 1-3 business days.
