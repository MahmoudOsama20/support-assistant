# Support Priorities and Response Times

## Priority levels
Every support request is given a priority from P1 to P4. The priority decides how quickly we make a first response and the target time for resolving the case.
- P1 (outage or unauthorized transactions): first response within 15 minutes, resolution target of 4 hours, handled 24/7.
- P2 (a major feature is impaired, for example you cannot send money): first response within 1 hour, resolution target of 8 hours, handled from 08:00 to 24:00.
- P3 (a general issue): first response within 8 business hours, resolution target of 3 business days.
- P4 (an inquiry or feedback): first response within 24 hours, resolution target of 7 business days.

## Contact channels
You can reach support in three ways. In-app chat is open from 08:00 to 24:00. The hotline 19800 is available 24/7. You can also send an email to help@nilewallet.example.

## Choosing the right channel
If you see transactions you did not make, or the service is down, call the hotline straight away so the case can be handled as P1. For a general question or feedback, chat or email is usually enough. If you suspect unauthorized use of your card, freeze it in the app first.
