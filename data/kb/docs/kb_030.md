# Business Onboarding

## Documents required
To open a business account, submit four documents: the commercial register, the tax card, the national ID of the authorized signatory, and the details of a settlement bank account. The settlement account is where the money from your sales is paid out, so make sure it is in the business's name.

## Review time
Nile Wallet reviews a business application within 5 business days. If a document is unclear or missing, the application may take longer, so check that every file is complete and readable before you submit.

## Limits and fees
An approved business wallet has a daily limit of 1,000,000 EGP. The merchant fee is 1.0% per transaction, the same rate as for other merchants.

## After approval
API keys are issued only after your business is approved. You can then connect your systems using the API, and test first in the sandbox described in the API and webhooks guide.
