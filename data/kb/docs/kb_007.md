# Lost, Stolen or Frozen Cards

## Freeze a card instantly
If you cannot find your card or suspect misuse, open Cards, choose the card and tap Freeze. The freeze takes effect immediately and is free. A frozen card declines all purchases and withdrawals, and you can unfreeze it at any time from the same screen.

## Report a lost or stolen card
If the card is lost or stolen, report it in the app (Cards > Report lost or stolen) or call the hotline 19800, which is open 24 hours a day. The card is blocked at once and can never be reactivated, so freeze it instead if you may still find it.

## Replacement cards
A replacement physical card costs 100 EGP and arrives within 3 to 5 business days. A virtual replacement card is issued instantly at no charge, so you can keep paying online while you wait.

## Unauthorized transactions
Report any purchase you did not make within 48 hours of the transaction. If you report within that time, you are not liable for the amount. Reports made after 48 hours are reviewed case by case. To contest a charge, see the disputes article.
