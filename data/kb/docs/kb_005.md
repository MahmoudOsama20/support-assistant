# Fee Schedule 2026

Effective 1 January 2026. This schedule replaces the 2025 fee schedule. All amounts are in Egyptian pounds (EGP).

## Everyday fees
- Wallet-to-wallet transfer: free.
- Instant bank transfer: 0.5% of the amount, with a minimum of 3 EGP and a maximum of 15 EGP.
- ATM cash withdrawal: 5 EGP.
- Cash withdrawal at a partner outlet: 10 EGP flat.
- Cash deposit at a partner outlet: free.

## Monthly account fee
Basic accounts pay nothing. Plus accounts pay 15 EGP per month and Premium accounts pay 40 EGP per month. The monthly fee is charged on the first day of each month.

## Cards
A virtual card is free. Issuing a physical card costs 75 EGP, and replacing a lost or damaged physical card costs 100 EGP.

## Foreign currency
Card payments made in a foreign currency include a 1.5% currency conversion markup on top of the exchange rate. International transfers have their own fee, described in the international transfers article.

## Other charges
There is no fee to open an account or to receive money. The app always shows the fee before you confirm a transaction.
