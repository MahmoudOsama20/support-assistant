# Phishing and Official Channels

## What Nile Wallet will never ask
Nile Wallet never asks for your OTP, PIN, password or full card number, whether by phone, SMS, chat or email. It also never asks you to move money to a "safe account". This applies on every channel and at any time, even if the caller knows your name or some details of your account. Anyone who asks for these things is not from Nile Wallet, however convincing they sound.

## How to recognize official channels
Our official domain is nilewallet.example, and SMS messages from us come from the sender name NileWallet. The app should only be installed from the official app stores. Before entering your details on any page, check that its address matches our official domain exactly. Treat links, domains or sender names that look similar but differ in any detail as suspicious.

## Reporting suspicious messages
Use "Report suspicious message" in the app, or forward the message to report@nilewallet.example. Do not reply to the sender or open any links in the message. It also helps to delete the message after reporting it so you do not tap it by mistake.

## If you shared a code or clicked a link
Act immediately. Freeze your account and card in the app, call the hotline 19800, and change your password. Then tell support exactly what you shared.
